UFO Sightings Across Eras (SQL)

Used SQL to group UFO sightings into four time periods and compare how many there were, how strange they were rated (0 to 15), and how long they lasted.

Tools: SQL, SQLite, Python, pandas

EraSightingsAvg strangenessAvg duration
Before 19002146.0929.0
1900-19491,2556.3411.7
1950-197912,3736.6417.4
1980 and later4,2746.5719.0

Key findings

  • Sightings peaked in 1950-1979, with almost ten times as many reports as 1900-1949.
  • Average strangeness stayed between about 6.1 and 6.6 in every era, so reports became more common, not stranger.

Strangest UFO Sightings by Location (SQL)

Used SQL to find which types of locations have above-average strangeness ratings, keeping only locations with at least 100 sightings so small samples don't skew the results.

Tools: SQL, SQLite, Python, pandas

Key findings

  • Every location that beat the overall strangeness average is a rural, natural, or industrial setting.
  • Wetlands and forests had the highest strangeness, about 7.0 out of 15.
  • Pasture and farmlands had the most sightings, together about 28% of all reports.
