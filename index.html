Projects

Hospital Admissions by Province (SQL Implicit Joins)

Used an implicit join across three tables in a practice hospital database to find out how many admissions came from each province, and how many different patients were behind those admissions.

Tools: SQL (SQL sandbox)

Key findings

  • Ontario had the most admissions, with 94 total admissions from 62 unique patients.
  • Alberta and Nova Scotia had the fewest, with 1 admission each, and British Columbia and Saskatchewan followed closely with 2 each.
  • There is a massive gap between Ontario and every other province, so admissions in this dataset are heavily concentrated in one place.

Cancer Patient Admissions (SQL Subqueries)

Used a subquery with IN to find every patient admitted to a practice hospital with a cancer diagnosis. The inner query finds the patient IDs, the outer query pulls their names and cities, and each patient is listed once even if they were admitted more than once.

Tools: SQL (SQL sandbox)

Key findings

  • The query returned 5 patients admitted with a cancer diagnosis.
  • The patients came from 5 different cities, and no city appeared more than once.
  • This is a practice dataset, so the results describe the sandbox and not real-world patients.
